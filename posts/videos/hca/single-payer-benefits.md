For <a href="https://healthcareforall.org/">Health Care for All -
California</a>, this video explains the benefits Single Payer will have to
seniors. Single-payer healthcare provides even more comprehensive benefits than
Medicare, and it will improve access and lower costs by pooling state and
federal funds. Directed by Hector Ceballos, Written by Clifford J. Tasner,
Produced by Clifford J. Tasner and Ricky de Laveaga, Cinematography by Vincent
Toto, Edited by Ricky de Laveaga, Music by Clifford J.&nbsp;Tasner
