Californians now have two tracks forward to achieve Single Payer Healthcare. SB
770 requires the state of California to design a new, guaranteed, universal
healthcare system and address questions that people have about how it all works.
You can participate in speak outs about healthcare around the state, and write
letters to your legislator about your experience with the healthcare system and
what you want changed.<!--more--> Visit
[Healthy California Now](https://healthyca.org) to find out more about CalCare
and how to make it happen. Written and Directed by Clifford J. Tasner, Produced
by Clifford J. Tasner and Ricky de Laveaga, Cinematography and Editing by
Vincent Toto, Graphics by Ricky de Laveaga, Music by
