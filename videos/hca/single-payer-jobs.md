For <a href="https://healthcareforall.org/">Health Care for All -
California</a>, this video inoculates the audience against medical-industrial
complex propaganda which falsely asserts that Americans can count on the
healthcare they get through their jobs. Single-payer healthcare means you have
great lifelong health care not tied to a job. Directed by Hector Ceballos,
Written by Clifford J. Tasner, Produced by Clifford J. Tasner and Ricky de
Laveaga, Cinematography by Vincent Toto, Edited by Ricky de Laveaga, Music by
Clifford J.&nbsp;Tasner
